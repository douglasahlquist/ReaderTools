@grammar 开始 | 开始 and 结束: beginning and end
> **开始** can stand alone (电影开始了) or take a verb after it: 开始下雨, 开始学习. **结束** is its opposite: 课结束了. Both often go with 以前, 以后 and 的时候.
电影 开始 以前， 我们 吃 了 饭。 | Before the movie started we ate.
课 结束 以后， 我们 继续 说话。 | After class ended we kept talking.

@grammar 其实 | 其实: actually
> **其实** tells the real situation, usually after something that seems different: 看起来很难，其实不难. It goes at the start of a sentence or before the verb.
大家 都 说 很 远， 其实 很 近。 | Everyone says it's far, but actually it's close.

@grammar 只是 | 就是 and 只是
> **就是** points to exactly this one: 就是他 “it's him”. After a good point, **就是** or **只是** add a small “but”: 很好，就是太贵 / 只是有点儿贵. **只是** also means “merely”: 只是问问.
这 个 房间 很 好， 就是 小 了 一点儿。 | This room is nice — just a little small.
我 不 是 不 想 去， 只是 没 时间。 | It's not that I don't want to go; I just don't have time.
